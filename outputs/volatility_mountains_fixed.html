Smooth animation interpolates between real historical frames; intermediate frames are visual transitions, not actual dates. Cinematic mode applies a percentile-clipped monotonic height transform and lighting, and may hide subtle extrema; no extra peaks or random noise are added. Frames show nonconsecutive UTC dates; animation between dates is visual interpolation, not real observations. This is descriptive volatility, not model robustness or predictive evidence. This renderer is self-contained and operates offline. Optimized 2× rendering stride and 20 FPS cap to improve compatibility; original historical data and numerical calculations are unchanged.
